# Ask, Don't Search: An AI Business Assistant, Business System Included

## 1. Problem: Productivity Bottleneck

Most growing companies face the same daily friction, whether they run on spreadsheets or already have business software.

**If you run on spreadsheets, chat groups and paper:**

- **Data is scattered.** Sales are in one spreadsheet, purchases in another and invoices in email. Nobody has the full picture.
- **The same work is done twice.** A sales order is typed into a spreadsheet, then again into an invoice, then again into the books.
- **Month-end closing is slow and stressful.** Numbers have to be collected, matched and corrected by hand.
- **Assets and stock go untracked.** Nobody is sure what the company owns, where it is or what it's worth.

**If you already use business software:**

- **It's hard to use.** Screens are crowded, menus are deep, and staff need many clicks to answer a simple question.
- **It doesn't fit local needs.** The software uses foreign terms, has incomplete Indonesian translation, and doesn't match local tax rules or document formats.
- **Only a few people really know how to use it.** Everyone else asks them, so they become a bottleneck.
- **Licence costs keep rising.** Many systems charge per user, so adding staff costs more every year.

**In both cases, the result is the same:** your people spend hours each week looking for information instead of acting on it.

## 2. Solution: AI Business Assistant

We provide an **AI Business Assistant** your team can talk to in plain Bahasa Indonesia or English, **bundled with a complete business management system** it runs on.

**Think of it as ChatGPT or Gemini, but for your business system.** Your team chats with it the same way, but instead of general knowledge it answers from your company's own up-to-date data. It follows your access rules and sticks to business topics.

**One system for the whole business.** Accounting, purchasing, sales, inventory and assets all live in one place. A sale entered once updates stock, invoices and the books automatically.

**Built for Indonesian companies.** Screens, terms and documents are in clear Bahasa Indonesia. Tax and reporting are set up for local regulations.

**An AI Business Assistant inside the system.** Instead of searching through menus, staff just ask:

| "Sales by customer this month vs last month" | A side-by-side comparison that highlights big changes |
| "Show me the profit and loss report" | The report, summarised, with a link to the full version |
| "Create a sales order for PT Maju Jaya" | A sales order form with the customer already filled in, ready to check and save |

| "Print invoice INV-0045" | A ready-to-download PDF |

**What this means for productivity:**

- **Answers in seconds, not hours.** Staff get numbers themselves instead of waiting for someone in Finance.
- **Less dependence on a few experts.** New and non-technical staff can use the system from day one.
- **Less double entry and fewer errors.** Data is entered once and flows everywhere it's needed.
- **Faster decisions.** Managers see up-to-date figures whenever they ask.

**Safe by design:**

- **Each person sees only what they're allowed to see.** The assistant uses the same access rights as the rest of the system, including limits such as "only my branch". A sales staff member can't use it to read payroll or bank details.
- **The assistant never changes data on its own.** New documents open as pre-filled forms the person checks and saves. Actions and setup changes show a preview first, and nothing happens until the user clicks Apply. Access is checked again at that moment, and an unconfirmed preview expires after 10 minutes. Your normal approval rules still apply.
- **Every request is recorded.** You can always see who asked for what and whether it was allowed. Field values are never written to the log, and AI usage is tracked per user.
- **Your data isn't used to train AI.** The assistant uses a business-grade AI service that doesn't use your data to train its models. Only the information needed to answer a question is sent, and your records stay in your own system.
- **It sticks to your business.** The assistant only helps with your company's data and processes. It politely declines anything else.

## 3. How It Works

```
   Staff member asks a question
   ("which invoices are overdue?")
                │
                ▼
   The assistant works out what is needed
                │
                ▼
   It checks: is this person allowed to see this?
          │                          │
         Yes                         No
          │                          │
          ▼                          ▼
   Finds the answer in your    Explains politely that
   business data               access isn't allowed
          │
          ▼
   Replies in the chat with a summary, table
   or link to the actual document
          │
          ▼
   The request is recorded in the activity log
```

1. **Ask.** A staff member opens the chat inside the system and types a question in everyday language.
2. **Understand.** The assistant works out what the person wants: to find data, open a report, start a new document or check an approval.
3. **Check access.** Before showing anything, it checks that person's access rights, the same rights they have everywhere else in the system.
4. **Answer.** It replies with a clear summary, a table or a direct link to the document, report or form.
5. **Record.** Every request is logged so management keeps full oversight.

**Example: closing the month**

- **Today:** Finance asks each team for their spreadsheets, matches payments against invoices by hand, and chases missing documents over chat.
- **With the assistant:** The finance manager asks "Which customer payments this month aren't matched to an invoice yet?", gets the list with links to each document, and works through it. For the management meeting, they ask "Compare this month's expenses with last month" and get a ready summary.
