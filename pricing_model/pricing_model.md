# **Pricing Model**

Here is the revised complete pricing architecture. The **Base AI Token Allowance** remains at your established baseline (500 / 2,000 / 5,000 queries), and a dedicated **Extra AI Token Allowance Fee** is introduced for clients who run out of monthly queries.

## 1\. Subscription Packages (Monthly & Annual SaaS)

All plans include **100% full module access** (Accounting, Buying, Selling, Stock, HR/Payroll, Manufacturing) and **full Indonesian localization** (Indonesian COA, e-Faktur export, PPN 11–12%, local bank formats, e-Materai).

| **Included Seats** | 5 Seats | 15 Seats | 30 Seats |
| **Monthly Option** | IDR 1,600,000 / mo | IDR 4,200,000 / mo | IDR 7,500,000 / mo |

| **Base AI Token Allowance** | 500 Queries / mo (Company Pool) | 2,000 Queries / mo (Company Pool) | 5,000 Queries / mo (Company Pool) |
