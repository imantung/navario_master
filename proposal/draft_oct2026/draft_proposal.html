01
Problem

Productivity Bottleneck

Most growing companies face the same daily friction, whether they run on spreadsheets or already have business software.

If you run on spreadsheets, chat groups and paper

  • Data is scattered.Sales are in one spreadsheet, purchases in another and invoices in email. Nobody has the full picture.
  • The same work is done twice.A sales order is typed into a spreadsheet, then again into an invoice, then again into the books.
  • Month-end closing is slow and stressful.Numbers have to be collected, matched and corrected by hand.
  • Assets and stock go untracked.Nobody is sure what the company owns, where it is or what it's worth.

If you already use business software

  • It's hard to use.Screens are crowded, menus are deep, and staff need many clicks to answer a simple question.
  • It doesn't fit local needs.The software uses foreign terms, has incomplete Indonesian translation, and doesn't match local tax rules or document formats.
  • Only a few people really know how to use it.Everyone else asks them, so they become a bottleneck.
  • Licence costs keep rising.Many systems charge per user, so adding staff costs more every year.
In both cases, the result is the same: your people spend hours each week looking for information instead of acting on it.

03
Process

How It Works

From question to answer in five steps, with access checked at every step.

1Ask

A staff member opens the chat inside the system and types a question in everyday language, for example "Faktur mana saja yang sudah lewat jatuh tempo?"

2Understand

The assistant works out what the person wants: to find data, open a report, start a new document or check an approval.

3Check access

At every step, it checks that person's access rights, the same rights they have everywhere else in the system.

4Answer

It replies with a clear summary, a table or a direct link to the document, report or form.

5Record

Every request is logged so management keeps full oversight.

Example: closing the month

Today

Finance asks each team for their spreadsheets, matches payments against invoices by hand, and chases missing documents over chat.

With the assistant

The finance manager asks:

Pembayaran pelanggan bulan ini mana saja yang belum dicocokkan dengan faktur?

They get the list with links to each document, and work through it. For the management meeting, they ask:

Bandingkan biaya bulan ini dengan bulan lalu

and get a ready summary.
