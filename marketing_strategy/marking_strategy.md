# **Marketing Strategy (TBD)**

To successfully launch and scale your ERPNext consulting business as a solo founder in Indonesia, your marketing strategy must solve the single biggest friction point for local SME owners: **trust and implementation risk**.

Because Indonesian business culture relies heavily on relationships (*silaturahmi*), social proof, and clear ROI, a multi-channel inbound and outbound strategy tailored to local buying habits is most effective.

## 1\. Position as a "Localized Problem Solver," Not a Software Reseller

Indonesian SMEs do not buy ERP software because of features; they buy to solve specific operational and tax headaches.

* **Anchor Strategy:** Position your messaging around **Tax Compliance & Control**.  
* **Key Hooks:**  
  * *"ERPNext Lokal Indonesia: Siap e-Faktur, PPN 11–12%, dan Laporan Keuangan Standar Akuntansi Indonesia."*  
  * *"Stop kebocoran stok dan selisih kas toko/pabrik tanpa perlu bayar Ratusan Juta untuk software legacy."*  
* **Target Segments First:** Focus on **Wholesale Distributors, Light Manufacturing, and Multi-Branch Retail/Trading** in Jabodetabek, Surabaya, and Bandung. These sectors feel the most pain with inventory leaks and tax reporting.

## 2\. High-Intent Inbound & Social Proof (Digital Channels)

SME decision-makers in Indonesia actively research tools on LinkedIn, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

* **LinkedIn & Founder Branding (Personal Authority):**  
  * Share teardowns of messy SME operations: *"Bagaimana 1 Distributor di Cengkareng Mengurangi Stockout 40% dalam 3 Minggu."*  
  * Post before-and-after workflow screenshots showing custom e-Faktur export or automated PPN calculations.  
* **YouTube / TikTok (Demonstrate, Don't Just Tell):**  
  * Publish short, practical 3–5 minute video walk-throughs in Bahasa Indonesia (e.g., *"Cara Export e-Faktur Langsung dari ERPNext"* or *"Cara Monitoring Stok Multi-Gudang via Hp"*).  
  * Feature your **In-System AI Assistant** to showcase modern technology that local competitors (Accurate/Jurnal) lack.  
* **Search Engine Marketing (Google Search Ads):**  
  * Target high-intent commercial keywords: `jasa implementasi erpnext indonesia`, `konsultan erpnext jakarta`, `alternatif accurate online murah`, `software erp manufaktur indonesia`.

## 3\. Community & Ecosystem Distribution (Leveraging Channels)

As a solo consultant, leverage existing networks rather than building an audience from scratch.

* **Accounting & Tax Consultant Partnerships (Referral Engine):**  
  * Local tax consultants (*Konsultan Pajak*) often struggle when their SME clients give them messy Excel files.  
  * Partner with local accounting firms or freelance accountants and offer a **10% recurring referral commission** or reciprocal business for bringing you implementation clients.  
* **Local Business Associations:**  
  * Engage with communities like **HIPMI**, **APINDO**, or local SME chambers of commerce.  
  * Offer to conduct a free 45-minute webinar/workshop titled: *"Digitalisasi Laporan Finansial & Gudang untuk UKM Menghadapi Audit Pajak."*

## 4\. Direct WhatsApp Outreach & High-Converting Funnel

Indonesian commercial deals close on WhatsApp.

  \[ Simple Landing Page / WhatsApp Click-to-Chat \]  
                     │  
                     ▼  
  \[ Free 15-Min System Readiness Call or AI Assessment \]  
                     │

* **The WhatsApp Hook:** Add a direct `wa.me` CTA button on every page and proposal document: *"Konsultasi Gratis Readiness ERP dengan Konsultan."*  
* **Low-Friction Entry:** Offer the **Fast-Track Onboarding (IDR 3.5M)** as a low-risk productized service to get clients in the door quickly, then upgrade them to higher tiers or paid add-ons later.

* **Week 1:** Set up a clean 1-page landing page featuring your **3-tiered pricing table** and WhatsApp contact button.  
* **Week 2:** Create 3 short video walk-throughs of Indonesian localized features (e-Faktur, Stock Audit, AI Assistant).  
* **Week 3:** Reach out to 15 local tax/accounting consultants in Jabodetabek to introduce your partner/referral program.  
* **Week 4:** Launch a small Google Search Ad campaign (IDR 1M–2M monthly budget) targeting high-intent ERP queries.
